BREAKFAST

Besan Chilla

Enjoy your Besan Chilla as a nutritious breakfast option!

NUTRITION

250 cals
1 serving
11g Protein 18%
3g Fat 11%
36g Carbs 58%

INGREDIENTS

FOR 1 serving
  • 40 g - besan (gram flour)
  • 1 · small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 · tomato, chopped
  • 0.25 cup · 50 g - carrot, grated
  • 0.25 cup · 50 g - chopped bell peppers
  • 1 · green chillies, chopped (adjust to your spice preference)
  • 0.5 · 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped, for garnish
  • 0.5 tsp · 2.5 g - cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp · 2.5 g - turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • water
  • Cooking oil or ghee for greasing the pan

HOW TO PREPARE

In a mixing bowl, combine besan, chopped onions, tomatoes, grated carrots, chopped bell peppers, green chili, grated ginger, and fresh coriander leaves.
Add cumin powder, turmeric powder, and salt to the mixture. Mix well.
Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and grease it with oil or ghee.
Pour a ladleful of the batter onto the pan and spread it into a circle. Cook until the bottom is golden brown, then flip and cook the other side.
Serve hot with chutney or yogurt.
