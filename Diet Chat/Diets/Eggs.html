NUTRITION

155 cals
1 serving (100g)
13g Protein 52%
11g Fat 44%
1g Carbs 4%

INGREDIENTS

FOR 1 serving
  • 2 large eggs
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste
  • Optional: herbs or cheese for added flavor

HOW TO PREPARE

Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk until well combined. Add salt and pepper to taste.
Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat. If desired, add a small amount of butter or oil.
Pour the whisked eggs into the pan. As the eggs begin to set, use a spatula to gently push the cooked edges toward the center of the pan.
When the eggs are mostly set but still slightly runny on top, fold the omelet in half (if making an omelet) or scramble gently until fully cooked.
Serve immediately, adding any additional herbs or cheese if desired.
OR just boil the egg and only consume the egg white and eat only one whole egg per 200gm.
