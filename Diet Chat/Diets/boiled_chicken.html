NUTRITION

165 cals
1 serving (100g)
31g Protein 75%
3.6g Fat 20%
0g Carbs 0%

INGREDIENTS

FOR 1 serving
  • 100g chicken breast
  • Water
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: herbs and spices (e.g., bay leaf, peppercorns, thyme)

HOW TO PREPARE

Place chicken breast in a pot and cover with water. Add salt and any optional herbs or spices.
Bring the water to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for about 12-15 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through.
Remove the chicken from the water and let it rest for a few minutes before serving.
