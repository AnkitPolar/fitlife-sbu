Besan Chilla

Enjoy your Besan Chilla as a nutritious breakfast option!

NUTRITION

11g Protein 18%
3g Fat 11%
36g Carbs 58%

INGREDIENTS

FOR 1 serving
  • 40 g - besan (gram flour)
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 0.25 cup (50 g) - carrot, grated
  • 0.25 cup (50 g) - chopped bell peppers
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • 0.5-inch piece of ginger, grated
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • 0.5 tsp cumin powder
  • 0.5 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Water
  • Cooking oil or ghee for greasing the pan

HOW TO PREPARE

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine all the ingredients.
  2. Add spices and mix well.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan and grease it with oil or ghee.
  4. Pour a ladleful of the batter onto the pan and spread it into a circle.
  5. Cook until golden brown on one side, flip, and cook the other side.
